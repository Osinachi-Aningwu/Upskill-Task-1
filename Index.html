My Favorite Animal

I like capybaras because they are so calm, gentle, and rarely aggressive,
which is why so many animals (and even humans) love being around them.

3 Cools Facts about capybaras

  1. They are the biggest rodents in the world, growing up to 4 feet long and weighing 35–65 kg.
  2. Capybaras are semi-aquatic experts — they love swimming, diving, and even sleeping in water.
  3. They have webbed feet, which make them excellent swimmers.
